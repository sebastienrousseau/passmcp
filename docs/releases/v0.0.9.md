## Highlights ⭐️

* **Capability-gated protocol probes and SEP-2164 unknown-resource checks (139 checks total)**: tool-only protocol probes skip when `capabilities.tools` is absent instead of passing on `-32601` (ADR-0002), stateless `2026-07-28` first contact selects a declared list RPC, and `protocol.unknown_resource` verifies `-32602` with `error.data.uri` on missing resources.
* **SEP-2549 cache hints across tools, resources, and prompts**: `catalog.cache_hints` inspects `tools/list`, `resources/list`, and `prompts/list` via `Client.ListResourcesWithHints` and `Client.ListPromptsWithHints`, validating `ttlMs >= 0` and `cacheScope` in `{"public", "private"}` on declared endpoints.
* **Immediate RFC 6761 `.invalid` egress refusal and conformance guide**: `internal/egress` answers `502 Bad Gateway` before DNS lookup on `.invalid` hosts so stub resolvers cannot stall fault-injection runs, and `docs/migrating/from-mcp-conformance.md` plus `docs/COMPARISON.md` document how passmcp pairs with `@modelcontextprotocol/conformance`.
* **In lockstep with the passmcp family at 0.0.9**: requires `satellion.com/passmcp-reporting` `v0.0.9`, updates `go.yaml.in/yaml/v3` (`v3.0.6`) and `github.com/spf13/pflag` (`v1.1.0`) in `SBOM.md`, and floors `certifi` in the manual build lock.
