## Highlights ⭐️

* **Six Apache-2.0 MCP language SDKs join the lockstep family**: `passmcp-sdk-go`, `passmcp-sdk-python`, `passmcp-sdk-java`, `passmcp-sdk-cpp`, `passmcp-sdk-typescript`, and `passmcp-sdk-rust` enter the released `0.0.10` manifest under [ADR 0013](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/passmcp/blob/main/docs/adr/0013-language-sdks-are-apache-satellites.md) with `2025-06-18` through `2026-07-28` conformance and offline `in-toto` attestation verification.
* **Portfolio-dogfooded catalog and PII precision**: `catalog.toxic_combination` excludes closed-world bundled-fixture readers (`openWorldHint: false`), `catalog.tools.annotation_honesty` accepts read-only inspection and simulation `run_*` / `execute_*` tools while rejecting command/script/migration runners, and `telemetry.Redactor.KeepIdentifier` prevents server and catalogue names from self-masking when echoed under `"name"`.
* **Upgraded to `satellion.com/passmcp-reporting` v0.0.10**: `go.mod`, `go.sum`, `SBOM.md`, install snippets, and the generated 15-repository family table move in lockstep to `0.0.10`.
